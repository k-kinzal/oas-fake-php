final class OperationDefinition
Public API: explicitly declared with @visibility public.

Binds an OpenAPI operation to its path, method, and resolved inheritance context.

Routing identity cannot be changed after indexing. Request and response schemas remain the parsed OpenAPI objects, without exposing the entire source operation.
